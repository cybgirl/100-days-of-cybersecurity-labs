- Limits the spread of threats within a network

- **Firewalls** – Control and filter traffic

---

## 🔥 Firewalls & Their Classifications

Firewalls can be classified based on the **OSI layers** they operate on:

- **Packet Filtering Firewalls (Layer 3 – Network Layer)**  
  Filter traffic based on IP addresses and ports  

- **Stateful Inspection Firewalls (Layer 4 – Transport Layer)**  
  Track active connections and make decisions based on session state  

- **Proxy Firewalls (Layer 5–7 – Session to Application Layers)**  
  Act as intermediaries, inspecting traffic at a deeper level  

- **Next-Generation Firewalls (NGFWs)**  
  Advanced firewalls that include:
  - Deep Packet Inspection (DPI)  
  - Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS)  
  - Application awareness and control  

---

## 🔎 Security Insight

- They monitor and regulate network traffic  
- Serve as the **first line of defense** against external threats
