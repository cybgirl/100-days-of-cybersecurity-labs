# Day 39 - Incident Response & Management

**Focus:** Incident Response (IR) and Incident Management (IM) via TryHackMe labs.

## Key Takeaways

### Incident Response vs Incident Management
- **Incident Response (IR):** Technical, real-time actions to contain and recover from a security incident.  
- **Incident Management (IM):** Strategic framework for reporting, escalation, documentation, and continuous improvement.

### Roles During an Incident
- **First Responders:** Initial triage and evidence collection  
- **SOC Analysts:** Monitor, detect, and analyze incidents  
- **CERT (Computer Emergency Response Team):** Investigate incidents further  
- **CSIRT (Computer Security Incident Response Team):** Handle high-alert incidents  
- **CMT (Crisis Management Team):** Manage full-scale cyber crises  
- **Incident Managers:** Coordinate communication and response actions  
- **Executives & Legal Teams:** High-level decision-making and compliance oversight

### Incident Management Lifecycle
1. Preparation  
2. Detection & Analysis  
3. Containment  
4. Eradication  
5. Recovery  
6. Lessons Learned

### Common Pitfalls
- Delayed detection due to poor monitoring or alert fatigue  
- Lack of communication causing confusion or duplicated efforts  
- Skipping documentation, making audits and lessons difficult  
- Ignoring post-incident reviews, risking repeat mistakes  
- Not following playbooks, potentially worsening the incident

> **Insight:** Effective incident response relies not only on tools, but also on clear coordination, communication, and calm decision-making.
